Lysozyme in water: research overview for this sample
=====================================================

This overview was written for the Microsoft Discovery GROMACS sample. It
introduces the research topic and points to the open-access papers in this
folder. It is educational context, not a literature review or validated
scientific guidance.

1. The protein
--------------

Hen egg-white lysozyme (HEWL) is a small, well-characterized enzyme of 129
amino acids with four disulfide bonds. It is one of the most studied model
proteins in structural biology and biophysics, with many crystal structures,
NMR measurements and simulation studies available. This sample uses the public
PDB entry 1AKI (chain A) as its starting structure.

2. Why water matters
--------------------

Proteins in solution are surrounded by a hydration layer of water molecules
that interact with the protein surface through hydrogen bonds and
electrostatics. Water screens charges, stabilizes the folded structure and
takes part in conformational changes. Removing water, or modelling it only
implicitly, can change the protein's shape and dynamics. Several papers in this
folder study these effects directly:

- Explicit water versus no water or implicit solvation in simulations of HEWL
  (Pechlaner et al., 2022).
- How the amount of water changes whether thermal unfolding of lysozyme is
  reversible (Phan-Xuan et al., 2021).
- How external electric fields affect the dipoles of lysozyme and its
  hydration layer (Wu et al., 2022).

3. Molecular dynamics of lysozyme
---------------------------------

Molecular dynamics (MD) simulations calculate how atoms move over time using a
force field. For lysozyme in water, MD is used to study its flexibility, its
conformational substates and how it unfolds under stress:

- Long simulations in explicit solvent show how lysozyme explores its
  conformations over time scales from nanoseconds to microseconds
  (Wang et al., 2015).
- Simulations at high temperature and low pH show faster unfolding and the
  early formation of beta strands linked to amyloid aggregation
  (Zein et al., 2022).

4. How this sample studies it
-----------------------------

The sample's GROMACS tool runs a short, educational simulation of lysozyme in
water: OPLS-AA protein parameters, SPC/E water, neutralizing ions, a
dodecahedron box, energy minimization, 100 ps equilibration at constant volume
then constant pressure, and 100 ps of production at 300 K and 1 bar. It reports
the backbone RMSD, the radius of gyration and thermodynamic properties. These
runs are far too short to establish equilibrium or support formulation
conclusions; they demonstrate the workflow.

Useful questions to explore with this knowledge base:

- Why is explicit water important when simulating lysozyme?
- How does hydration affect lysozyme's stability and unfolding?
- What time scales are needed to observe lysozyme's conformational changes?
- Which conditions promote lysozyme unfolding or aggregation?
